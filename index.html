HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

  • W A S D — Drive the fortress
  • MOUSE — Aim the main turret
  • LEFT CLICK — Fire primary weapon
  • RIGHT CLICK — Fire secondary weapon
  • SPACE — Defensive ability (overshield)
  • SHIFT — Overdrive boost
  • TAB — Tactical zoom-out
  • ESC / P — Pause

THE LOOP

  • Survive escalating waves of hostiles.
  • Destroy enemies to earn scrap.
  • Between waves, bolt on new modules — every one physically appears on your fortress.
  • Stack upgrades into absurd synergies.
  • Clear the biome boss, roll to the next wasteland.
  • Earn cores to permanently upgrade.

TIPS

  • Keep moving — a stationary fortress is a dead fortress.
  • Watch your POWER — energy weapons and shields drain it.
  • Build a theme: go all-in on missiles, lasers, or drones.
  • Chain kills to build a combo multiplier on scrap.
  • Boss weak points glow — hit them during phase transitions.
