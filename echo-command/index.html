HOW ECHO COMMAND WORKS

THE LOOP

Each mission runs on a fixed timer. When it expires the timeline resets — the arena, enemies, and objectives all restore. But your previous run is replayed perfectly by a holographic Echo. You are not correcting mistakes; you are building an army of your past selves.

WINNING

All objectives must be completed within a single loop. Split the work across timelines: this loop hacks Terminal A, the next covers Terminal B, the one after holds the door.

ECHOES ARE FIXED

Echoes repeat exactly what you did — every step, every shot, every miss. If an echo dies, it dies at that same moment in every future loop. Protect them.

CONTROLS

  • MOUSE Aim · LMB Fire
  • SHIFT Sprint
  • SPACE / F Gadgets
  • Q / E Rotate camera · WHEEL Zoom
  • MMB DRAG Free-look / tilt
  • T (hold) Reset the timeline early
  • ESC Pause

FRIENDLY FIRE

Explosions damage everything — including your echoes. Choreograph accordingly.
